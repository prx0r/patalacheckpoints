sarve cāsya vaśyā bhavanti
bhave bhave nātibhave
adbhireva śucirbhavet
ṣaṇmāsānnityayuktasya
sarvaviśiṣṭo'yaṃ panthāḥ
siddhayogī na lipyate karmaṇā pātakena vā
darśanaśravaṇamananavijñānāni cāsya pravartante
ekaḥ kṣemī san vītaśokaḥ
yady avekṣed yady abhibhāṣet
hṛdi kurvīta dhāraṇām
sukṛtaṃ ca teṣāmādatte
balapramathanāya namaḥ
namaste astu rudrarūpebhyaḥ
ṛṣir vipro mahāneṣaḥ
sarvāṇi dvārāṇi pidhāya
unmattavadeko vicareta loke
tan no rudraḥ pracodayāt
sarvabhūtadamanāya namaḥ
sarvatra cāpratihatagatir bhavati
yena paribhavaṃ gacchet
atredaṃ brahma japet
āpo vāpi yathākālamaśnīyādanupūrvaśaḥ
māṃsam aduṣyaṃ lavaṇena vā
māyayā sukṛtayā samavindata
sarveṣāṃ cāvaśyo bhavati
athātaḥ paśupateḥ pāśupataṃ yogavidhiṃ vyākhyāsyāmaḥ
yathālabdhopajīvakaḥ labhate rudrasāyujyam
vāmadevāya namo jyeṣṭhāya namo rudrāya namaḥ
chittvā doṣāṇāṃ hetujālasya mūlam
ghoraghoratarebhyaśca
na kaścid brāhmaṇaḥ punarāvartate
apasavyaṃ ca pradakṣiṇam
bhūyiṣṭhaṃ sampravartate
hasitagītanṛttaḍuṃḍuṃkāranamaskārajapyopahāreṇopatiṣṭhet
gūḍhavidyā tapaānantyāya prakāśate
nānyabhaktistu śaṃkare
mahādevasya dakṣiṇāmūrteḥ
bhasmanā triṣavaṇaṃ snāyīta
mūtrapurīṣaṃ nāvekṣet
indro vā agre asureṣu pāśupatamacarat
asanmāno hi yantrāṇāṃ sarveṣāmuttamaḥ smṛtaḥ
godharmā mṛgadharmā vā
sadā rudramanusmaret
ubhayaṃ tu rudre devāḥ pitaraśca
īśvaraḥ sarvabhūtānām
pāpaṃ ca tebhyo dadāti
kalavikaraṇāya namaḥ
sarveṣāṃ cāvadhyo bhavati
atitaptaṃ tapastathā
kṛtānnamutsṛṣṭam upādadīta
sarveṣāṃ cānāveśyo bhavati
sārvakāmika ityācakṣate
paribhūyamāno hi vidvān kṛtsnatapā bhavati
tasmādubhayathā yaṣṭavyaḥ
sa teṣāmiṣṭāpūrtamādatta
sthāpayitvā ca rudre
ity etair guṇair yukto bhagavato mahādevasya mahāgaṇapatir bhavati
anena vidhinā rudrasamīpaṃ gatvā
amaṅgalaṃ cātra maṅgalaṃ bhavati
tato 'sya yogaḥ pravartate
