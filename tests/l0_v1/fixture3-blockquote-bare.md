## The bare prose

> "And-one's-own-self-he-should-offer (svātmānaṃ ca nivedayet)."

Here, again, the blue and the rest, being the not-abandoned blue-form, manifesting, are of the
non-difference (abheda). The Lord's spontaneity (svācchandya), being of the essence of
consciousness, reflects (vimṛśati) too.
