## The long lemma

The manifestation of the knower-and-known is of the non-difference-from-the-manifestation
(prakāśābheda-maya), and, [being] the essence of consciousness (saṃvido vimarśa-paryantatvāt),
reflects (vimṛśati) itself [even] as-so-manifesting, [i.e.] by the own-form (svarūpa).
