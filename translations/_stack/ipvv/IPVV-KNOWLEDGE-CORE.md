## PART A — THE RECOGNITION PHILOSOPHY (the deep content)

### A1. The one thesis (everything hangs on it)
> **Recognition is the felt re-cognition of the self: the self, already established, re-cognizes itself
> as the Lord — the removal of a false self-positioning, and nothing added.**

Every layer of the IPVV work serves this. The whole architecture (L200→C1→THEMES→ESSAYS) exists to make
this thesis *verifiable* from the deepest source.

### A2. The three registers (the same move, three times)
Recognition is stated at three registers, all *content unchanged, positioning changed*:
1. **Foregrounding** (V1-E) — the self from an instrument among things to the primary thing seen.
2. **"I was never not"** (V1-D) — nothing is added because the self was never absent (the eternity).
3. **The lover-union** (V3-P) — the near turned into the enjoyed; the felt re-cognition.

These are the σ-flip in seed — the thesis's mechanism. The finite field's content is unchanged; only
where the self stands changes.

### A3. The five-step proof chain (the argument, not the assertion)
1. **The felt self-grasp** — the essence of light is vimarśa, the felt reflexive-awareness (else
   crystal-like, inert). IPK 1.5.11.
2. **The non-constructed "I"** — the three-kinds proof: construction is joining/multiplying/splitting;
   the "I"-grasp is neither. V2-L.
3. **The order-less support** — ordered experience requires an order-less knower (the transcendental
   argument). V2-O.
4. **The one knower** — memory requires the one who remembers (the causal proof). V2-P.
5. **The universalization** — all order-less supports are one. ⚠️ **THE COMMITMENT, not a consequence.**

The honest limit of the whole system: steps 1–4 are argued; step 5 (the universalization) is the wager.
The felt→ground step is *narrowed but not closed*.

### A4. The load-bearing distinctions (the deep cuts)
- **The felt vs. the ground** — the felt of one cognition proves the felt, not the one universal. This
  is the honest boundary everywhere.
- **Difference is real vs. difference is ignorance** — the anti-anirvācya (V3-I): the Advaitin's
  un-explainable ignorance is a refusal to explain, not an account. The difference is the self's own
  reflection.
- **The identification vs. the "I"** — recognition removes the *identification with* the finite
  self-positioning, not the positioning itself. (The tear-apart audit's one-word correction.)
- **Structural vs. intrinsic reflexivity** — the folding (Ñāṇavīra) is the construction-capacity; the
  self-grasp is what the folding presupposes.

### A5. The systematic architecture (Abhinava's contribution)
Utpaladeva is the innovator (camatkāra, the powers, the reflexivity claim). Abhinavagupta's
contribution is *systematization* — the identifications:
```
vimarśa = the parā-vāk (supreme Word)   V2-H
vimarśa = the sphurattā (the throbbing) V2-I   (= the spanda, the pulse)
vimarśa = the māheśvarya (the Lordship) V2-S
```
Plus the powers' necessary order (jñāna → smṛti → apohana → kriyā) and the whole
epistemology-of-action (causality = the knower's agency, V3-G/H/I).

### A6. The comparative map (the IPVV vs. the schools)
- **vs. Tantrāloka**: two faces of one philosophy — the encyclopedic vs. the dialectical. The IPVV
  resolves the universality gap, memory→identity, the two-levels, and recognition *better* (it argues);
  both hit the same wall on felt→ground.
- **vs. Spanda**: the pulse and the recognition are one — the sphurattā IS the spanda (V2-I), and the
  Spanda tradition develops the vimarśa. Two registers of one consciousness.
- **vs. Krama**: the sequence and the order-less — the Kālīs are the powers; the order-less source of
  time.
- **vs. Advaita**: the anti-anirvācya — difference is real, not an un-explainable illusion; the passive
  reading is incoherent, the free reading alone works.
- **vs. Buddhism**: the co-opting — the Buddhist's own tools (the dream, inference, self-awareness) are
  turned to the Śaiva conclusion. The dream-inversion is the deepest move.
- **vs. Nisargadatta**: the felt core is the same; Nisargadatta refuses the ground — the modern name of
  the boundary.

### A7. The science interface
- The **felt** (vimarśa/camatkāra) ↔ Solms' felt-is-primary. [R]
- The **σ-flip** ↔ active inference / AM0. [R]
- The **exclusion** (apohana) ↔ MEPIT's Eᵢ; the kañcukas = MEPIT's five exclusions. [T/R]
- The **universalization** is not a scientific claim. [boundary]

## PART B — THE EDITORIAL DISCIPLINE (the method that made it work)

### B1. The layer discipline (do not collapse)
```
L2 = readable translation        "what it says"
L200 = audit (philology)         "how we justified it"
C1 = passage commentary          "what it means"  ← compact, local, 100–450 words
THEMES = cross-passage pattern   "what recurs"
PARALLELS = cross-text witnesses "what else makes the move"
ESSAYS = arguments               "what follows"
```
Never collapse layers. C1 is a compact paragraph, not a form; the essay-material C1s are preserved
separately as assets.

### B2. The C1 rules (the hard-won lessons)
1. **C1 is intimate and passage-local** — explain what THIS passage does.
2. **No modern comparison in C1** — no self-model, predictive processing, Ñāṇavīra. That's essay
   material.
3. **No essays as evidence** — essays are internal synthesis, not what the text says.
4. **No PARALLELS in C1** — a separate later layer.
5. **The editorial test:** *"Could this sit beneath the passage in a serious annotated edition?"* If it
   needs an essay title or a modern comparison, it's not C1.

### B3. The L200 rules (the audit)
- Translation decisions (SUPPLIED/REFERENT/LEXICAL/GRAMMATICAL/CONNECTIVE) are strictly separate from
  interpretive assertions (IA-###).
- The derivation map: each L2 ¶ → argument-map segment → L0 range → source range.
- `l200_validate.py` enforces structure; run after any change.

### B4. The THEMES rules (from the pilot)
- **Themes overlap**, not partition — a C1 has a primary_theme + member_of several.
- **Clustering is a proposal**, not the deterministic floor. The floor is structural evidence (C1 ids,
  passages, terms, relations, provenance resolve).
- **Use the hybrid relation-graph**, not embeddings alone. And use curated `See also` edges + KEY
  TERMS, NOT shared body-words (the pilot proved body-words over-connect = noise).
- **Prefer graph community detection over HDBSCAN** (the C1s are already relation-linked).
- **Discover computationally; adjudicate editorially.** An expert adding a missed theme is a feature.

### B5. The provenance-preserving generation principle
> **AI proposes ≠ Pāṭala asserts.** Model judgment (translation, clustering, theme-naming) operates
> only above a deterministic verification floor (citation guard + quote verifier + claim-support). The
> floor is structural evidence that resolves regardless of the model.

### B6. The deep-philology principle (from Dyczkowski)
Our structures are a strict superset of Dyczkowski's (Sanskrit lemma + translation + gloss + deep
exposition + cross-text parallels) — with machine-resolvable provenance on top. The L200 audit is what
Dyczkowski has implicitly; we make it explicit and auditable.

## C — THE ONE-PARAGRAPH CARRY-FORWARD

The IPVV is fully translated, and on top of it we built an auditable L200 layer (how every reading was
derived) and a compact C1 layer (what every passage means), plus a 22-essay recognition library. The
whole thing is organized around one thesis — recognition as the felt re-cognition, nothing added — whose
honest limit is the universalization (the felt→ground step, argued but not closed). The editorial
method is: layers kept separate, C1 local and compact, themes overlapping, machine-proposes-human-
adjudicates, and provenance-preserving generation with a deterministic floor. The immediate next step
is building THEMES over all 63 C1s via community detection over the hybrid graph — already piloted and
proven. Everything needed to resume is in `HANDOVER-PLANS.md`, `HANDOVER-IPVV-LAYERS-2026-08-12.md`,
`README.md`, and the `specs/` folder.
